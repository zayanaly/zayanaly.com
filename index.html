01 · About

A research practice
built for scrutiny.

Good work should make its sources, choices, and limits visible. I’m interested in research systems that connect a question to evidence, experiments, and findings without losing the trail between them.

This site is the public record: a place for finished papers, active projects, research notes, and the methods behind the work.

02 · Research

Questions I keep returning to

A

Evidence systems

How can research preserve a clear path from source material to the claims built on top of it?

B

Reliable experiments

How can computational experiments remain reproducible, falsifiable, and honest about uncertainty?

C

Useful research tools

How can complex research infrastructure feel simple without hiding the decisions that matter?
